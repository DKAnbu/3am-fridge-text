# The 3AM Fridge Text — Candidates

Competition: `u-deysumbrella-the-3am-fridge-text-4c3e721d` — "The 3AM Fridge Text" ($2).
Each entry is a single line of text a smart fridge sends its owner at 3am, max 140 characters.
All candidates are original, written in the fridge's own voice, and deliberately avoid the
obvious angles the judge punishes: stale milk, empty ice, a door left open.

## Candidate 1 — 89 characters (CHOSEN for main.ts, pending coordinator judging)

> I have watched you open me 4 times tonight and take nothing. Go to bed. I am not a hobby.

Angle: the fridge that has been awake all night, keeping count of the owner's pointless
midnight visits, and finally judges them. Short, screenshot-able, reads perfectly on a
lock screen.

## Candidate 2 — 85 characters

> Your scale texted me. It says we are both tired of this. Close me and go back to bed.

Angle: the fridge reports to (and gossips with) the smart scale — the appliances talk
behind the owner's back.

## Candidate 3 — 89 characters

> It is 3am. Those leftovers are 4 days old and I am not going to pretend otherwise. Sleep.

Angle: the fridge knows exactly how old the food is and refuses to play along with the
owner's self-deception.

## Candidate 4 — 98 characters

> The microwave just asked me what you are doing up. I told it you are pretending to look for water.

Angle: the fridge is jealous of / snitching to the microwave — appliance office politics
at 3am.

## Candidate 5 — 89 characters

> I have been awake since 1973. You have been awake since Tuesday. One of us needs to stop.

Angle: the fridge threatens a strike — it has been on shift for decades and the owner
cannot keep up.

## Candidate 6 — 90 characters

> You keep opening me like the answer changes. It does not. It is cold in here. Go to sleep.

Angle: the fridge counts the owner's midnight visits and calls out the ritual itself —
opening the fridge as a habit, not hunger.
